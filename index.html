How to use this studio

01 · Set the goal
Need 100 leads? Enter your CPL or CPC and conversion rate to estimate required spend.

Enter actual campaign results and inspect cost, conversion and lead-quality metrics.

Sample data is fictional and designed for practice. Forecasts are estimates based on the assumptions you enter, not guaranteed outcomes.

01 / Lead goal budget planner

Work backwards from a business target. Enter the number of leads required and either your expected CPL, or the CPC and click-to-lead conversion rate.

Estimated budget₹1,65,000Illustrative default: 100 leads × ₹1,500 CPL + 10% buffer.
The planner will show expected clicks, base media spend, buffer and daily budget.

Formula: Budget = target leads × expected CPL. If using funnel inputs: required clicks = target leads ÷ click-to-lead CVR; budget = required clicks × CPC. Buffer is added separately. Use a realistic historical or test-derived CPL/CPC and conversion rate.

04 / Funnel intelligence

Review where the funnel is losing volume. Enter counts from the same cohort and reporting period wherever possible.

How to interpret

Impressions → clicks: inspect targeting, creative, offer, placement and CTR.
Clicks → landing page views: inspect page speed, redirects, tracking and click quality.
Landing page views → leads: inspect message match, form friction, trust and offer clarity.
Leads → MQLs → SQLs: inspect audience fit, qualification criteria, lead response time and sales feedback.
Opportunities → wins: inspect sales process, pricing, product fit and attribution.

05 / Scenario simulator
