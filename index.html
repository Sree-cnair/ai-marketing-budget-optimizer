CASE 02 / D2C · E-COMMERCE
224% → 92%
D2C TEA BRAND · ILLUSTRATIVE CASE

Turning an ACOS problem into a product-level growth plan

A sample case study demonstrating how product economics, wasted search spend and checkout friction can combine to distort blended advertising efficiency.

MOCK DATA · OCT–JUL

The diagnosis

In this illustrative scenario, blended ACOS moves from 224% to 92% over ten months while monthly GMV grows from ₹1.6L to ₹5.9L. The April spike to 177% triggers a product and funnel review.

  • Spend concentrated in products with high ACOS; Peach Green Chai reached 417% and Kashmiri Kahwa 263% in the mock April data.
  • Efficient products were underfunded; Assam Kadak showed 9% website ACOS in the scenario.
  • Checkout abandonment was 61.7%; only 38% of checkouts converted to orders.
  • 98% of sessions and 93% of orders were mobile, making mobile checkout experience critical.

Four-part response

  • Reallocate budgets toward products below the target ACOS; set product-level guardrails.
  • Remove waste through search-term negatives, match-type separation, exclusions and geo/dayparting analysis.
  • Improve conversion with mobile checkout simplification and abandoned-cart recovery.
  • Raise average order value with bundles and free-shipping thresholds; scale only after efficiency holds.

Scenario outcome: by July, website ACOS 95%, Amazon India 89%, Amazon USA 84%; blended ACOS 92%. GMV ₹5.86L against ₹5.39L ad spend. All figures are hypothetical mock data from the supplied illustrative case study, not actual results.
