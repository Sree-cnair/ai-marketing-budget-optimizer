01 / THE CHALLENGE & APPROACH

Build visibility. Capture intent. Qualify demand.

The plan was designed to reach enterprise decision-makers and generate potential sales opportunities for managed office solutions. The strategy separates upper-funnel reach from lower-funnel lead acquisition, while keeping sales qualification visible in the forecast.

05 / AUDIENCE STRATEGY & CAMPAIGN STRUCTURE

Segment by intent and business context

Meta · Awareness

Geographic and demographic targeting, business decision-maker profiles and company-related signals across Bengaluru, NCR and Hyderabad in the supplied structure.

LinkedIn · Awareness

Job seniority, industry, company size and job title; product-led and industry-led audience groups; thought-leadership, case-study and carousel formats.

Lead-gen audiences

Lookalike or predictive audiences based on SQL, sales and broker/IPC datasets, alongside broader professional and industry-based segments.

06 / MEASUREMENT & LEAD QUALITY

Follow the funnel beyond the form fill

Attention

Reach · Impressions · CPM · Frequency · CTR

Acquisition

Clicks · CPC · Leads · CVR · CPL

Lead quality

MQLs · SQLs · SQL rate · Cost per SQL

Business impact

Opportunities · Cost per opportunity · Pipeline contribution

Optimisation

Reallocate budget by channel and audience quality; test creative and landing-page variants.

Sales feedback

Review lead acceptance, response time, disqualification reasons and opportunity progression.

07 / OPTIMISATION ROADMAP

Turn the plan into a learning system

01 · Creative angles

Test workspace flexibility, speed-to-occupancy, enterprise experience and location-led messaging.

02 · Audience quality

Compare seniority, industry, company-size and first-party lookalike segments using cost per SQL.

03 · Landing experience

Test lead form length, proof points, location pages and stronger calls to action.
