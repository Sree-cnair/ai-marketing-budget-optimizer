01 / BUSINESS CONTEXT

Marketing a considered enterprise decision.

Table Space offers managed office solutions to businesses. The marketing challenge is not simply to generate enquiries; it is to communicate the proposition clearly, reach relevant business audiences and support a path from initial interest to a meaningful sales conversation.

Audience understanding

Understand the business context, target organisations, decision-makers and the needs behind an enterprise workspace requirement.

Clear product communication

Support content that explains the managed-office proposition, locations, flexibility and enterprise value.

Demand support

Connect search, content and campaign inputs with the broader objective of building visibility and supporting qualified enquiries.

From brief to coordinated marketing activity.

02 · Campaign planning support

Contribute to campaign briefs, channel discussions, media-planning inputs and coordination with external partners across digital channels.

03 · Content and product communication

Support website and marketing content that communicates enterprise managed-office solutions and aligns messaging with business priorities.

03 / SEO & SEARCH

Build discoverability around relevant business intent.

My SEO & Search contribution includes:

  • Website content

  • SEM inputs
  • Search visibility

The focus is to support stronger search relevance and help connect the business offering with the language and intent of potential enterprise customers.

04 / PAID MEDIA & DEMAND GENERATION

Support the plan; keep the objective in view.

My involvement includes supporting campaign planning and coordination for enterprise lead-generation activity across LinkedIn, Google and Meta. The work calls for aligning audience inputs, messaging, channel roles and reporting with the quality of enquiries—not treating lead volume as the only measure.

LinkedIn

Professional audience and decision-maker targeting inputs for B2B communication.

Google

Search intent and SEM inputs to support demand capture.

Meta

Campaign coordination and audience/creative inputs as part of the broader channel mix.

05 / MEDIA-PLANNING EXAMPLE

Translating a business brief into a channel mix.

One illustrative B2B media-planning exercise used a ₹20 lakh planning envelope to organise awareness and lead-generation investment across Meta, LinkedIn and Google. The value of the exercise was in structuring the funnel, articulating channel roles, documenting assumptions and defining how performance and lead quality could be assessed.

Awareness planning

Considered reach and visibility through Meta and LinkedIn, with channel roles mapped to the intended business audience.

Lead-generation planning

Considered LinkedIn, Google and Meta against their respective audience and intent roles.

Measurement approach

Look beyond CPL to MQLs, SQLs, cost per SQL and sales feedback when assessing lead quality.

Important context: ₹20 lakh is the illustrative budget in a planning exercise, not a claim that I independently managed or spent this budget, nor a statement of verified campaign results. Forecasts from the planning exercise are not presented as delivered outcomes.
06 / HOW I THINK ABOUT MEASUREMENT

Connect marketing activity to business quality.

Visibility

Search visibility, reach, impressions and engagement signals.

Acquisition

Clicks, enquiries, conversion rate and cost per lead.

Lead quality

MQL-to-SQL progression, cost per SQL, sales acceptance and feedback.
