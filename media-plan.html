01 / BUSINESS CONTEXT

Marketing a considered enterprise decision.

Table Space offers managed office solutions to businesses. The marketing challenge is not simply to generate enquiries; it is to communicate the proposition clearly, reach relevant business audiences and support a path from initial interest to a meaningful sales conversation.

Audience understanding

Understand the business context, target organisations, decision-makers and the needs behind an enterprise workspace requirement.

Clear product communication

Support content that explains the managed-office proposition, locations, flexibility and enterprise value.

Demand support

Connect search, content and campaign inputs with the broader objective of building visibility and supporting qualified enquiries.

02 / MY STRATEGIC CONTRIBUTION

Think strategically. Execute collaboratively.

Campaign planning & execution

Contribute to campaign strategy and planning, and support the running of campaigns across digital channels—from coordination and setup inputs through ongoing execution.

Content & coordination

Work on website and marketing content, coordinate with internal stakeholders and external partners, and help keep marketing activity aligned with business priorities.
