01 / THE BUSINESS CHALLENGE

Improve efficiency without putting growth on hold.

The task was to improve advertising efficiency while supporting stronger D2C sales. Looking only at blended account metrics would not show where budgets needed attention, so the work focused on product-level performance, campaign structure and the journey from ad interaction to purchase.

Understand product economics

Review product-level performance to identify where investment needed to be reduced, protected or rebalanced.

Improve campaign efficiency

Review campaign structures and retargeting to make spend more purposeful across the paid media mix.

Address conversion friction

Look beyond clicks and spend to understand the conversion funnel and opportunities to improve the path to purchase.

02 / WHAT I WORKED ON

Four connected optimisation levers.

01 · Product-level budget allocation

Used product-level performance to guide budget allocation and improve the efficiency of media investment.

02 · Campaign structures

Worked on campaign structure improvements to support clearer control and optimisation across platforms.

03 · Retargeting

Supported retargeting activity to reconnect with audiences who had already shown interest.

04 · Conversion funnels

Reviewed conversion journeys to identify opportunities to reduce friction between ad engagement and purchase.

03 / CHANNEL SCOPE

Performance work across the paid media mix.

Meta Ads

Campaign performance and retargeting within the broader D2C acquisition mix.

Google Ads

Search and paid acquisition activity as part of cross-channel performance management.

Amazon Ads

Marketplace advertising performance included in the blended efficiency view.

04 / OUTCOME & LEARNING

Efficiency and growth can be managed together.

Over 10 months, blended ACOS reduced by 59%, monthly GMV grew by 266%, and monthly ad spend reduced by 23%. The experience reinforced the value of looking beyond platform-level clicks and evaluating product economics, campaign structure and conversion performance together.
