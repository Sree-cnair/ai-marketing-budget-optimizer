01 / THE DIAGNOSIS

Blended performance hid very different product realities.

Unprofitable spend concentration

Peach Green Chai at 417% ACOS and Kashmiri Kahwa at 263% ACOS consumed 56% of website spend in the mock April data.

Efficient products underfunded

Assam Kadak showed 9% website ACOS and 36% Amazon India ACOS; Vanilla Almond showed 54% ACOS on Amazon USA.

Checkout leakage

61.7% checkout abandonment; only 38% of checkouts converted to orders in the illustrative April scenario.

02 / THE FOUR-LAYER STRATEGY

Fix the economics before scaling.

01 · Reallocate by product

Reduce spend on products with persistently high ACOS; move budget towards products with stronger contribution and a sustainable return.

02 · Remove wasted spend

Use negative keywords, match-type separation, audience exclusions and search-term reviews to reduce non-converting traffic.

03 · Improve conversion

Recover abandoned checkouts, simplify mobile purchase journeys and improve product content and trust signals.

04 · Scale winners

Refresh creative, test bundles and raise budgets gradually only after products sustain target efficiency.

03 / THE TREND

ACOS improvement was not linear.

The illustrative series improves through February, spikes in April as demand drops after peak season, then declines below 100% by July following product-level diagnosis and optimisation.

05 / KEY LEARNINGS

What the exercise demonstrates.

Diagnose below the blended metric

Product-level and channel-level breakdowns can reveal winners and loss-making spend hidden in account averages.

Conversion is a media lever

Improving checkout completion can create more orders from traffic already paid for.

Scale only with evidence

Increase investment gradually after efficiency is sustained, rather than chasing volume immediately.
